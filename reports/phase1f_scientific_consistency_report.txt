1. R5 SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION & RECONCILIATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Physical Definition:
    R5 is the radius at which azimuthally averaged tangential wind at 850 hPa decreases to 5 knots.
    It is an objective satellite-based measure of outer tropical cyclone size (Knaff et al. 2014).
  * Dimensional Structure:
    - Dimensions:  ('R5_time',)
    - Units:       degrees_latitude (~111 km per degree)
    - Lengths:     Varies by storm lifetime (e.g. 15 to 80 time steps along storm track)
    - Values:      Range [2.0, 28.0] deg lat (Mean: 10.5 deg lat ~ 1,070 km)
  * Separation from Radial Profile 'radius':
    In previous EDA drafts, R5 was erroneously described as a 5-bin radial spatial image array.
    CORRECTION: The variable 'radius' is a separate 30-element coordinate (2 to 118 km, units km)
    used solely as bin centers for 'brightness_temperature_mean_radial_profile'.
    R5 is a 1D time-series of storm vortex size, NOT a spatial image grid.
  * Modality Independence:
    - Overpasses with R5 present:            2429 (93.46%)
    - Overpasses with R5 but NO IRWIN image: 219 (6.43%)
    R5 is derived independently from operational wind size models; it remains available even
    when geostationary 2D imagery is missing.

9. SPATIAL GRID FEASIBILITY RECONCILIATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Geometric Inconsistency Identified:
    A 256x256 grid at 4 km has side length 1,024 km. From center to edge is 512 km.
    It CANNOT cover an 800 km radius in all directions (requires at least 1,600 km width).
  * Feasible Grid Candidates:
    1. Candidate B2: 400x400 Cartesian grid at 4 km (1,600 km domain; full 800 km radial coverage).
    2. Candidate C:  Multiresolution nested grids (256x256 at 2 km core + 256x256 at 8 km outer).
    3. Candidate D:  Storm-centered Polar grid (160 radial bins x 360 azimuthal bins; compact & natural).
  * Conservative Remapping Mandate:
    Precipitation fluxes (GPROF / Radar rain rate) MUST use first-order area-weighted conservative
    remapping to conserve rain mass. Bilinear interpolation is invalid for precipitation mass.
    Categorical masks (quality flags, surface type) MUST use nearest-neighbor interpolation.
